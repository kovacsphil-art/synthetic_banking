01 / The process · Methodology

One connected process.
Control at every handoff.

Measure first, review AI proposals, generate new records, then compare the result with the same original baseline.

Explore a stage: hover, focus with Tab, or tap. Press Escape to dismiss its explanation.

  1. The selected original tables establish one completed baseline. Their statistical profile anchors every later comparison.
  2. Original records are summarized into distributions, missingness and relationships. These aggregates guide generation without copying records.
  3. The AI model receives aggregate evidence and proposes relationships worth preserving. Row-level banking records are not supplied to it.
  4. A reviewer approves individual proposals. Only eligible approvals with a matching predefined implementation can affect generation; unsupported proposals are skipped.
  5. Measured profiles and eligible reviewed rules guide new identifiers and linked records. An execution audit records which proposals actually affected the dataset.
  6. Generated records are re-profiled and compared with the pinned original baseline. Structural checks and separate privacy screens distinguish fidelity from release suitability.
  7. A successfully validated execution produces a sanitized aggregate report for this site. Failed runs retain the last valid public report; no row-level datasets are published.

03 / AI & control · Methodology

AI suggests what matters.
Review determines what can run.

The model identifies candidate dependencies from aggregate statistical evidence. It does not generate customer records or execute its proposed text.

  1. 01

    Aggregate evidence

    Measured distributions and relationships, without row-level banking records.

  2. 02

    AI proposal

    A candidate dependency, grounded in the available statistics.

  3. 03

    Explicit review

    A human approves or rejects each proposal; approval alone is not execution.

  4. 04

    Predefined logic

    An approved, recognized implementation may run. Application is logged.

06 / Current maturity & next priorities

The controls are in place.
The next gains are in fidelity.

Orchestration, manual review gates, privacy screening, aggregate reporting and publication are implemented. Complex relationships remain simplified and validation coverage varies.

  1. 01

    Preserve more relationships

    Broaden measured numeric, categorical and temporal dependencies, with adequate evidence before adding controlled rules.

  2. 02

    Close material fidelity gaps

    Investigate tails, weaker correlations and date patterns. Calibrate validation tolerances for intended uses.

  3. 03

    Deepen privacy assurance

    Expand the existing disclosure screens with stronger attack-based testing. The current screen is not anonymization certification; no dataset download is enabled.
